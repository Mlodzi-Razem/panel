==== Context

The association is moving from Google Workspace to a Microsoft 365 nonprofit tenant.
MR Panel stays the single source of truth for members, bodies, districts and finances.
Microsoft 365 provides identity, mail and collaboration, and Panel synchronises it, the same way
it synchronises Google Workspace today.

The planned licence model:

[%autowidth]
|===
| Licence | Holders | Notes

| Business Basic (nonprofit grant)
| National bodies, IT commission, coordinators, active members, two harness service accounts
| 300 seats: 298 members and 2 service accounts. Mailbox, Office for the web with editing.
| Microsoft 365 F1
| All other members (about 300)
| 55 PLN per year gross. Teams, mail, SharePoint, Viva Engage, Office for the web, 2 GB OneDrive,
  Entra ID P1 and Intune. These members receive internal mail and are not expected to send it.
| Power Automate Premium
| The two harness service accounts
| Needed for the HTTP and Claude connectors.
| none
| Administrator accounts, two break-glass accounts
| Separate cloud-only accounts; break-glass accounts use FIDO2 keys.
|===

There are no B2B guest accounts for members. The external accounting office is the only guest.

The tenant uses Security Defaults and has no Conditional Access policies.
The authentication methods policy allows passkeys only (Microsoft Authenticator, iCloud and Google synced passkeys);
SMS, voice and e-mail codes are disabled. Passwords are random and unknown, self-service password reset is off,
and a lost passkey is replaced through a Temporary Access Pass issued after a video identity check.
Without Conditional Access the tenant cannot restrict downloads on unmanaged devices,
so member data is kept in Panel and nowhere else.

The register is _special category_ data under Art. 9 GDPR: membership of the association reveals
political opinions, and some members are minors: ordinary and supporting members from 16,
and supporting members younger than 16 who joined before the statute of 3 October 2026.
Every decision below assumes this classification.

==== Decisions

[cols="1,4,4"]
|===
| ID | Decision | Rationale

| AD-1
| Panel remains the system of record. No Dataverse, no commercial ERP.
| Open source (AGPL), runs on our own server, already models the statute.
  Dataverse requires Power Apps Premium licences. Dolibarr is the fallback finance back office
  if the finance module is not built.
| AD-2
| The Entra object ID (`oid`) identifies a user. It replaces the Google `sub`.
  Panel never keys a user on an e-mail address or UPN.
| `oid` is immutable and survives changes of licence, UPN and e-mail address.
  E-mail addresses and UPNs change during the migration.
| AD-3
| A third directory provider, `Entra`, is added next to `Mock` and `Google`. It uses Microsoft Graph.
| The reloadable directory (see <<reloadDirectroyImplementation>>) already supports
  switching providers. During the migration panel-web accepts both sign-in providers,
  and the directory provider is switched at the cutover.
| AD-4
| Panel gets an enrollment portal: Google sign-in, identity match, a one-time Temporary Access Pass,
  then a passkey in Microsoft Authenticator, iCloud or Google. Panel assigns Business Basic or F1.
| No passwords are created or handled. Accounts with administrative roles and members of the national
  bodies are excluded from the portal and are activated in person.
| AD-5
| Panel is published as a Teams app with static tabs: _Home_, _My membership_, _District_ (role-based)
  and _Votes_. It is pinned for everyone by an app setup policy and uses Teams SSO.
  The same manifest makes Panel available in Outlook and the Microsoft 365 app. Panel cards
  appear on the _Start_ home site, which is the tenant root site and, in Teams, the SharePoint app
  (formerly Viva Connections).
| Members find Panel where they already work. It requires `frame-ancestors` for Teams in the CSP
  and a token exchange in panel-web.
| AD-6
| There is no Teams team for all members. Panel publishes national announcements (resolutions, deadlines,
  election results) as news on the _Start_ home site and as posts in the _Ogłoszenia_ community in Viva Engage.
  District announcements go to the _Ogólny_ channel of the district team through Workflows.
  Viva Connections cards (SPFx) come later.
| Viva Engage is the space for the whole association. Panel is the source of the announcements, so they are audited.
| AD-7
| Districts, bodies and thematic circles map to Entra security groups that the directory synchronisation maintains.
  Working-group teams are public in the tenant and members join them themselves.
  Access is enforced in panel-api, which is the only place that holds member data.
| Group membership drives Teams and SharePoint access. The authorisation decision stays in Panel.
| AD-8
| A finance module: bank statement import (files), ledger, approvals by the CFO and a second board member,
  read-only access for the audit commission, UEPiK reports using the 2026 templates.
  Documents are stored in SharePoint with a retention policy.
| No maintained open-source tool supports UEPiK. Statements are imported from files,
  so Panel never holds bank credentials.
| AD-9
| Panel runs on the MR server (single-node microk8s), as it does today.
  The Azure nonprofit grant pays for backups (immutable Blob Storage), Logic Apps and Key Vault.
| Uses the existing Helm chart. Off-site backups survive the loss of the server.
| AD-10
| Voting uses Belenios on a separate host. An identity broker signs voters in with Entra ID and
  issues `sub` equal to the member number, which Panel writes to the user's `employeeId`.
  The voter list is exported from Panel. Trustees and the credential authority are not IT staff.
| Belenios uses the OIDC `sub` as the login, and Entra's `sub` is pairwise per application,
  so it cannot be known in advance. The broker reads the member number from Entra,
  so the voting host does not need a connection to Panel.
| AD-11
| The process harness (Power Automate with two Premium licences, Logic Apps) calls Panel's process
  endpoints and the Claude API. Prompts contain the minimum member data needed.
  No decision with legal effect is taken automatically.
| Art. 22 GDPR. The harness prepares, people decide.
| AD-12
| Panel has a _Pomoc IT_ form. Each request becomes a work item in Azure Boards, in an Azure DevOps organisation
  connected to the tenant's Entra ID and located in the EU. panel-api creates the item with a service principal
  and shows its state to the member. Simple questions go to the _Pomoc IT_ community in Viva Engage.
| Azure Boards gives the IT commission epics, stories, sub-tasks and sprints. Basic access is free for five users
  and Stakeholder access is free without a limit. Work items hold only the data needed for the request.
| AD-13
| A thematic circle has a public Viva Engage community. Members enrol in the circle through a form in Panel,
  and Panel maintains the membership of the circle's Teams team when the circle has one.
| A community created in Viva Engage has its own Microsoft 365 group with SharePoint, OneNote and Planner,
  but that group cannot also be a Teams team. The team is a separate group.
|===

The body templates follow the statute of 15 September 2024 (see the Members feature).
The proposed new governance structure would change the templates, but not this architecture.

==== Migration from Google Workspace

. Create the tenant with its final `onmicrosoft.com` name. Verify the main domain with a TXT record only,
  set it to _Internal Relay_ in Exchange, and add a routing subdomain with MX records pointing to Microsoft 365.
. Implement the `Entra` directory provider and the Entra sign-in provider. Run them next to Google.
. Create accounts with their final UPNs on the main domain: the six-digit member number is the UPN and the `employeeId`,
  and `imie.nazwisko` (`imie.N.nazwisko` on a collision) is the primary address. Accounts of current members are created
  as mail users, and the mail migration turns them into mailboxes. Panel links the Entra `oid` to the member
  through `employeeId` (phase 1 of the integration plan below).
. Pilot with the national board and the IT commission, then activate members in batches
  through the enrollment portal.
. Migrate mail, calendars and contacts with the Exchange Online Google Workspace migration,
  and Drive with Migration Manager.
. Cutover: switch the MX records, switch the directory provider to `Entra`, remove the Google sign-in provider,
  and keep Google Workspace read-only for 30 days.

==== Microsoft 365 Integration Plan

State on 21 November 2026:

* The tenant exists. Its SharePoint host is `mloderazemki.sharepoint.com`.
* Accounts follow the naming in the migration steps above. Accounts of current members are being created as mail users.
* _Start_ is the home site. The org-wide app setup policy in Teams pins the SharePoint app first.
* Panel signs in with Google only. It links a member through `directory_user_id` (the Google `sub`)
  or, on the first sign-in, through the domain e-mail.

The work is split into phases, in the order below. Each phase can be released on its own.

[cols="1,2,6"]
|===
| Phase | Scope | Details

| 0
| Entra app registration
| A single-tenant application _Panel MR_ with the web redirect URI
  `https://panel.mlodzirazem.org/api/auth/callback/azure-ad`.
  It exposes the API `api://panel.mlodzirazem.org/<client ID>` with the scope `access_as_user`,
  and the Teams, Outlook and Microsoft 365 app clients are pre-authorised for it.
  Graph application permissions are granted phase by phase, starting with `User.Read.All`.
  The credential is a certificate kept in Key Vault.

| 1
| Entra sign-in next to Google
| panel-web adds the Azure AD provider with the tenant-specific issuer.
  A table `member_identities(provider, subject, member_id)` replaces `directory_user_id`,
  so the Google `sub` and the Entra `oid` work side by side until the cutover.
  On the first Entra sign-in panel-api reads the user's `employeeId` from Graph by `oid`
  and links the member with that number. E-mail addresses and `preferred_username` are never used for linking (AD-2).

| 2
| Teams app
| The static tabs _Strona główna_, _Moje członkostwo_, _Okręg_, _Głosowania_ and _Informacje_ load `/teams/*` routes,
  which have no side navigation and take their theme from the Teams context.
  _Okręg_ shows district information to every member and pending change requests to coordinators.
  TeamsJS `getAuthToken()` sends the SSO token to `/api/auth/teams`. panel-web checks the issuer, audience,
  signature and scope and opens a session. There is no on-behalf-of exchange, because panel-web does not call Graph as the user.
  Inside the Teams frame the session cookie is `SameSite=None; Secure; Partitioned`.
  The CSP gets `frame-ancestors` for the Teams, Outlook, Office and `*.cloud.microsoft` hosts.
  The app is uploaded to the organisation catalogue and piloted with the national board and the IT commission
  through a separate setup policy. It is then pinned in the global policy, directly below the SharePoint app.

| 3
| Panel to Microsoft 365
| Activity feed notifications in Teams (dues, new votes, change requests to approve) use `TeamsActivity.Send`.
  The app is pinned by policy, so it is installed for every recipient.
  News for _Start_ is published with `Sites.Selected`, limited to that site (DF27).

| 4
| Entra directory provider
| After the migration Panel creates new accounts through Graph: the number as the UPN, `imie.nazwisko`,
  `employeeId` and a licence. It maintains the district and body groups (AD-7)
  and issues Temporary Access Passes in the enrollment portal (AD-4).

| 5
| Cards on _Start_
| SharePoint Framework Adaptive Card Extensions request a token for `api://panel.mlodzirazem.org/<client ID>`
  and call a panel-web endpoint that allows CORS from `mloderazemki.sharepoint.com`.
  This needs the tenant app catalogue and approval of API access in SharePoint. Until then _Start_ links to Panel.

| 6
| Cutover
| As in the migration steps: the Google sign-in provider and DF7 are removed, and `Entra` becomes the directory provider.
|===

==== Open Questions

These points must be verified in the pilot before they are relied on:

* Whether F1 users can use the custom Teams app and Viva Engage communities without restrictions.
* Whether Loop workspaces and the Viva Connections home site work for both Business Basic and F1 accounts.
* Whether Panel cards on the home site need SPFx Adaptive Card Extensions or can come from the Teams app.
* Whether Security Defaults require a Microsoft Authenticator push registration next to a passkey.
* Whether Temporary Access Pass and administrative units need Entra ID P1 in our scenario.
* Which claim Belenios 3.3 uses as the login, and whether the broker can issue `employeeId` as `sub`.
* Whether the Engage API can publish posts, or whether Workflows must be used.
* Whether community creation in Viva Engage can be limited to the IT commission.
* Whether the Outlook app can be pinned in Teams for F1 and Business Basic accounts by an app setup policy.
* Whether Stakeholder access in Azure Boards is enough for volunteers who triage requests.
* Whether F licences may be assigned to members of the association (frontline worker eligibility).
