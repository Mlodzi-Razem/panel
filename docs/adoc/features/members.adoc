== Members

The member register replaces the spreadsheet currently used by the national board.
It covers the register itself, district-board access, member self-service with change
requests, and a one-off import of the existing spreadsheet.
Recruitment is a separate feature built on top of it.

=== Organisation model

The model follows the statute of 15 September 2024 (articles referenced as _Art._).

==== Bodies

Every organ, unit and position is a `bodies` row created from a body template.
Templates and their member types (roles) are seeded; bodies are created by the national board.

Seeded bodies: the national organs above and 17 districts -- one per voivodeship
(dolnośląskie, kujawsko-pomorskie, lubelskie, lubuskie, łódzkie, małopolskie, mazowieckie,
opolskie, podkarpackie, podlaskie, pomorskie, śląskie, świętokrzyskie, warmińsko-mazurskie,
wielkopolskie, zachodniopomorskie) and _Zagranica_ (abroad) -- each with its District Assembly,
District Board and District Treasurer. Local circles are created later by the national board.

Who holds which role is stored in `body_memberships`
(member, body, member type, `valid_from`, `valid_to`).
A membership is active when `valid_from <= today` and `valid_to` is empty or in the future.

==== Rules on holding functions

Checked whenever a body membership is created:

* *Incompatibility (Art. 21)* -- ZK, KSK, KKR and KKW exclude each other, and ZK cannot be
  combined with ZO; KSK, KKR and KKW cannot be combined with ZO unless the ZO is single-person.
  The pairs are data (`body_template_incompatibilities`), not code.
  Assigning an incompatible function ends the earlier mandate (Art. 21.2): the panel shows the
  conflict and, after confirmation, sets `valid_to` of the earlier membership.
  An incompatible function starting on the same day or later than the existing one is rejected.
  Adding a second person to a single-person ZO whose member sits on KSK, KKR or KKW produces a
  warning, because that combination stops being exempt and has to be resolved by the national board.
* *Term limit (Art. 20)* -- more than 2 consecutive terms in the same body produces a warning,
  not an error; validating elections belongs to KKW.
* *Commissioner term (Art. 52)* -- at most 3 months, extendable twice; `valid_to` is required.

==== Membership of the association

Members are natural persons. Supporting membership of legal persons (Art. 7.4, 10.2) is out of scope.

* `membership_type`: `ORDINARY` | `SUPPORTING` | `HONORARY` (Art. 7.2).
* Ordinary membership requires age 16--26 at admission (Art. 9);
  supporting membership of a natural person requires age 14+ (Art. 10).
* Ordinary and supporting membership ends at the end of the calendar year in which the member
  turns 26 (Art. 17.1.2). The panel lists members whose membership ends this year;
  ending it stays a board action.
* Every member has exactly one active *primary* district and any number of *supporting*
  district affiliations without electoral rights (Art. 41.3--41.7), stored in
  `member_district_affiliations` (member, district, `PRIMARY` | `SUPPORTING`, `valid_from`, `valid_to`).
  Moving the primary district is decided by the board of the target district (Art. 41.4--41.5).

==== Permissions

Each member type points to a `permission_group`; a permission group grants `permission_type`
values through `permission_group_permissions`. The scope of the group decides where they apply:

* `GLOBAL` -- every district.
* `LOCAL` -- the district the body belongs to (the body itself for a district, otherwise its parent).
  A member falls under a district through their *primary* affiliation; boards of a
  *supporting* district get contact-data read access only.
* `BODY` -- the body itself only.

A user's effective permissions are the union over their active body memberships.
The national board can change the groups; the seeded defaults are:

|===
| Role | Scope | Permissions

| ZO Coordinator, ZO Vice-coordinator, District Commissioner

| contact and formal data read and manage
| District Treasurer

| `GLOBAL`

=== Linking a signed-in user to a member

The service token carries the Google account `sub` and e-mail.

. A member with `directory_user_id = sub` is the current user.
. Otherwise a member with `domain_email` equal to the token e-mail (case-insensitive) is linked
  by setting `directory_user_id = sub`, and the link is audited.
. Otherwise the user has no member record: they can sign in but see only an
  "account not linked" page.

Schema changes against `0.0.2`:

* `formal_name_first`, `formal_name_last` (`NOT NULL`); `formal_name` becomes a generated column.
* New nullable columns `pronouns`, `institution`, `social_profile_url`, `address_line`,
  `declaration_reference`, and `membership_type` (`NOT NULL`, default `ORDINARY`).
* Structured address columns (`address_country_code` ... `address_apartment`) become nullable;
  `address_line` holds the free-text address.
* `member_district_affiliations.votes_here` -- exactly one active affiliation per member has it;
  it defaults to the primary district (Art. 41.8).

Reading follows the same split: `CONTACT_PERSONAL_DATA_READ` exposes contact and naming fields,
`FORMAL_PERSONAL_DATA_READ` additionally exposes formal and organisational fields.
`formal_identifier` is returned masked (last 4 characters) unless the caller explicitly
requests it with `FORMAL_PERSONAL_DATA_MANAGE`; every unmasked read is audited.

=== Change requests

A member requests changes to formal fields; the request stores the proposed values and
a snapshot of the current ones.

* Status: `PENDING` -> `APPROVED` | `REJECTED` | `CANCELLED` (by the requester).
* Approved by anyone holding `FORMAL_PERSONAL_DATA_MANAGE` for the member's district
  (in practice their district board; the national board can too).
* Approval applies the values in the same transaction, unless a field changed since the
  snapshot -- then the approval fails and the approver must reject or re-check.
* At most one `PENDING` request per member.
* Nobody decides their own request.

=== Auditing

Every write (create, edit, self-edit, change-request decision, user linking, unmasked
`formal_identifier` read, body-membership change) produces an `audited_operations` row
containing the operation id, the acting user, the member id and the *names* of the changed
fields -- never the values.

=== Workspace synchronisation

The panel is the source of truth for board memberships.
Every body-membership change publishes a `BodyMembershipChanged` application event.
A future Google directory provider first reconciles all Workspace groups with the panel's
current state, then listens to these events (via Spring Modulith's event publication registry,
so they survive restarts) to apply changes incrementally.

=== API

All endpoints require the panel-web service token.

[%autowidth]
|===
| Method and path | Who | Purpose

| `PATCH /me` | any linked user | Edit own contact and naming fields
| `POST /me/change-requests` | any linked user | Request a formal-data change
| `GET /me/change-requests` | any linked user | Own requests
| `DELETE /me/change-requests/{id}` | requester | Cancel a pending request
| `GET /members` | `*_READ` in scope | List and search (`q`, `status`, `district`), paginated

| `GET /change-requests` | `FORMAL_PERSONAL_DATA_MANAGE` in scope | Pending requests to decide

=== User interface

* *User* tab -- own record, inline edit of contact and naming fields, "Request a change" for
  formal fields, list of own requests.
* *Members* tab -- visible only with any `*_READ` permission: searchable table filtered to
  the caller's districts, detail and edit views, and a "Change requests" inbox.
* *Bodies* view -- everyone can see the organs and who holds which function;
  the national board creates bodies and assigns functions, seeing incompatibility conflicts
  and term-limit warnings before confirming.

=== Spreadsheet import

A one-off, idempotent import of the current spreadsheet, exported as UTF-8 CSV.
It runs as a command-line mode of `panel-api`, always prints a dry-run report first,
and writes only when run again with `--apply`.

Matching: by `member_id` (column A, which has no header); otherwise by `domain_email`;
otherwise by `formal_identifier`. A row matching two different members is an error.

|===
| Column | Target | Notes

| A (no header) | `member_id` | Required
| Imię / Nazwisko | `formal_name_first` / `formal_name_last` | Required
| Preferowane imię / Preferowane nazwisko | `preferred_name_first` / `preferred_name_last` | Formal name when empty
| zaimki | `pronouns` |
| nr tel | `phone_numbers` | Split on `,` or `;`
| Prywatny adres e-mail | `private_emails` | Split on `,` or `;`
| Okręg stowarzyszenia | Primary district affiliation | Matched to a seeded district, ignoring case, diacritics and the word "okręg"
| Okręg wyborczy | Affiliation with `votes_here` | Adds a supporting affiliation when it differs from the primary district
| powiat / miasto / adres | `address_province` / `address_city` / `address_line` |
| uczelnia/pracodawca | `institution` |
| Data urodzenia | `birth_date` | `YYYY-MM-DD`, `DD.MM.YYYY` or `DD/MM/YYYY`

Every imported member gets `status = ACTIVE` and `membership_type = ORDINARY`.
Functions in bodies are not in the spreadsheet; the national board assigns them in the panel.
The report never prints personal data beyond `member_id` and the column name of each problem.

=== Electoral rights and Zeus

The National Electoral Commission (KKW) runs elections in https://zeus.grnet.gr[Zeus].
The register must be able to answer "who may vote in this election", so a future integration
only has to export it as a Zeus voter list:

* active ordinary members only (Art. 12.1, 15.1);
* without unjustified fee arrears longer than 3 months (Art. 14) -- known once fees exist;
* not suspended in membership rights (Art. 31.2.3, 33.2);
* for district elections: members whose *primary* district is that district (Art. 41.7);
  in delegate elections a member may vote in a supporting district instead (Art. 41.8).

The register also tells KKW which of its members hold a district function, since they may not
administer that district's votes and elections (Art. 39.6).

Electoral rights are computed, never stored, so they always reflect the current register.
The export itself (voter list file or Zeus API) is not part of this feature.
