== Membership fees

The fee register (_ewidencja składek_) is kept by the Treasurer of the national board (Art. 28.6).
The national board sets the amounts, discounts and exemptions (Art. 29.1.12).
This feature replaces the spreadsheet and the manual bank checks; the association's ledger and
UEPiK reports (AD-8 in the target architecture) are a later feature built on top of it.

=== Who pays

* Only *ordinary* members pay fees (Art. 13.4). Supporting and honorary members are exempt
  (Art. 15.3); a person in the introductory period holds supporting membership (Art. 10.3).
* A member owes a due for every calendar month in which they are an ordinary member with status
  `ACTIVE` or `SUSPENDED` on the first day of the month. The first due is for the month after
  `member_since` (or that month when `member_since` is its first day). Removal stops new dues;
  existing debt remains.

=== Rates and tiers

* The national board defines *fee tiers* (e.g. _Standardowa_, _Ulgowa_). Each tier has a history of
  monthly amounts, each with `valid_from` and the reference of the resolution that set it.
  The amount for a month is the one valid on the first day of that month.
* *The fee changes over time.* A new amount is added as a new rate with its own `valid_from`;
  the previous amounts stay in the history. A rate may start in any month for which no dues
  exist yet (normally the next month, or a later one when a resolution is entered in advance).
  Dues already generated keep the amount they were generated with, so a change never rewrites
  past months and a member's history always shows what was owed at the time.
* Every paying member is assigned to one tier (default: the tier marked as default).
  Assignments have `valid_from`, so changing a tier never rewrites past dues.
* No dues are generated until at least one rate exists.

=== Adjustments

Granted by the ZK Treasurer, with a reason category, a free-text justification and an optional
resolution reference, for a range of months (the end month may be left open until revoked):

[%autowidth]
|===
| Kind | Effect

| Exemption (_zwolnienie_) | No due is charged for those months.
| Discount (_ulga_) | The due for those months is reduced to a fixed amount.
| Deferral (_odroczenie_) | Dues stay owed, but arrears for those months are *justified* until the deferral ends.
|===

Reason categories: `FINANCIAL_HARDSHIP` (_trudna sytuacja materialna_), `HEALTH`, `OTHER`.

* *Exemption for financial difficulties.* The ZK Treasurer can exempt a member, fully or with a
  discount, because of financial hardship. The exemption may also cover past months: open dues in
  that range are cancelled, which also ends the arrears they caused (and restores electoral rights
  under Art. 14). Dues already paid stay paid; refunds are out of scope.
* *Confidentiality.* The reason category and justification are personal data about the member's
  situation. Only the ZK Treasurer (`FEES_MANAGE`) and the member themself see them; everyone else
  with `FEES_READ` sees only that an exemption, discount or deferral applies and for which months.
  The audit log records that an adjustment was granted or revoked, never its reason.
* Revoking an adjustment (or ending it earlier) affects only months from the revocation on;
  months before keep their adjusted dues.

Adjustments never change dues that are already paid; changing an unpaid due recalculates it.

=== Payments and allocation

* Sources: imported bank transactions (camt.053 or MT940 statement files) and payments recorded
  by the ZK Treasurer (cash, other). Only the ZK Treasurer records or matches payments.
* Every member has a personal *payment reference* `MR-<member number>-<check digit>`
  (the check digit is the ISO 7064 mod 11-10 digit of the member number), shown on their fees page
  together with the bank account and recipient name.
* Matching an incoming transfer: a valid reference in the title (case, spaces and dashes ignored)
  identifies the member; otherwise a sender IBAN previously confirmed for exactly one member does;
  otherwise the transfer waits in the *unmatched queue* for the Treasurer, who assigns it to one or more
  members (split) or marks it as not a fee. A manual match can remember the sender IBAN.
* A payment is allocated to the member's oldest open dues first; any surplus is a credit used by
  future dues. Reversing a payment (e.g. a wrong match) re-allocates the member's remaining payments.
* Importing the same statement twice changes nothing (transactions are identified by the bank's
  reference, or by account, date, amount and title when it is missing).

=== Arrears

* A due is *open* while it is not fully paid, and *overdue* after the last day of its month.
* A member's arrears start at the due date of their oldest overdue due that is not covered by a deferral.
* Arrears longer than 3 months suspend active and passive electoral rights (Art. 14).
  This is the `FeeArrearsPolicy` used by the electoral rights.
* Arrears longer than 4 months are grounds for exclusion by the national board (Art. 17.1.4b).
  The panel lists them; excluding a member stays a board decision.

=== Permissions

[%autowidth]
|===
| Role | Scope | Fees access

| ZK Treasurer | `GLOBAL` | `FEES_READ`, `FEES_MANAGE`: rates, tiers, adjustments, imports, matching, payments
| Other ZK roles | `GLOBAL` | `FEES_READ` (added)
| Audit commission (KKR) roles | `GLOBAL` | `FEES_READ` (added; read-only control, AD-8)
| District Treasurer | `LOCAL` | `FEES_READ` for the district
| District board (ZO) | `LOCAL` | `FEES_READ` for the district (added; arrears are needed for Art. 17.1.4c requests)
| Every member | own record | balance, history and payment instructions
|===

=== Auditing

Every rate, tier, assignment, adjustment, import, match, manual payment and reversal is audited
with ids and field names only; amounts are stored in the fee tables, not in the audit log.

=== User interface

* *Składki / Fees* for members: balance, next due, open dues, payment history, and how to pay
  (account, recipient, title with the personal reference).
* *Finanse / Finance* for `FEES_READ`: overview (expected vs collected per month), arrears list with
  the Art. 14 and Art. 17 thresholds, and each member's fee card (dues, payments, adjustments, balance).
  District roles see their district only.
* For the ZK Treasurer additionally: statement import (with a preview before saving),
  the unmatched queue, recording a payment, tiers and rates, and adjustments.

=== Out of scope
